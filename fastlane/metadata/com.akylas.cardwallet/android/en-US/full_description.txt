<i>OSS Card Wallet</i> is a free and Open Source app to transform your mobile device into a powerful card scanner and wallet<br><br><b>SCAN</b><br>• Scan your IDs, business cards, loyalty cards with a fast camera scan.<br>• Enjoy the automatic edge detection feature allowing you to quickly scan many documents<br>• Bulk scan to quickly add recto/verso<br>• Import cards from images or even existing PDF<br><br><b>BARCODE</b><br>• automatic detection of barcodes, qrcodes...<br>• manually create a card with a barcode<br>• add as many barcodes as you want to a simple card<br><br><b>PASSBOOK</b><br>• Full Passbook support<br>• import from .pkpass<br>• view your Passbooks as you would in the official Apple Passbook App<br>• Export and share your passbooks as .pkpass or PDF<br><br><b>ENHANCE</b><br>• Improve PDFs quality by applying enhancing filters for color or black & white cards.<br>• Edit crop<br>• Reorder pages, merge cards<br>• Rotate pages<br>• Change brightness and contrast to create beautiful cards<br><br><b>ORGANIZE</b><br>• Create folders with colors for easy access<br>• Sort cards<br>• Name cards<br><br><b>EXPORT</b><br>• create PDF directly from your mobile device by converting photos, images, other documents, into beautiful and configurable PDFs.<br>• export and share your cards to PDF or JPEG or PNG with your friends, family, colleagues<br><br><b>SEARCH</b><br>• OCR your cards using offline (to be downloaded first) and open source models in many languages.<br>• Generate searchable PDFs by converting images to text using the powerful OCR models from the Tesseract project.<br><br><b>SECURITY</b><br>• Improve the security of your generated PDF by adding a password.<br><br><b>SYNC</b><br>• Synchronize your cards, PDFs, Images with your computers, other devices or local folder sync.<br>• Sync using WebDAV, Google Drive, OneDrive<br><br><b>PRIVACY</b><br>• Use a free and Open Source card wallet to protect your privacy<br><br><b>COMMUNITY</b><br>• Provide feedback or even help translate the app. You are part of this project and you make help it grow so that everyone can enjoy the best free and open source
