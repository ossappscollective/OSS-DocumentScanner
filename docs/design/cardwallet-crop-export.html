Crop view (always dark editor) in four states and the export menu opened from its button in five contexts, on iOS (system menu) and Android (Material 3 Expressive menu), with handle and menu anatomy.

  • Crop view: an editor, so dark in both app themes (like the camera). The page sits in a 28 radius holder (#26262A) on a near black screen (#0E0E10); the area outside the outline is dimmed 55%. Round handles (corners 24, edges as 6 thick capsules), each with a 44 hit area; the closest one moves, relative to the finger. The magnifier (100, 2x) shows while a corner is dragged, top-left, flipping to top-right when the finger is there. Full image is one undo step.
  • iOS: glass close top-left, glass undo and redo top-right; system toolbar with the text button "full image" at the left and the prominent "done" at the right. Android: an edit mode, so every action is in the top bar: close, "crop", undo, redo, "done" text button, overflow with "full image". No bottom bar.
  • Export menu: one native menu replaces the two popovers (PDF and images). iOS: UIMenu from the export button, which opens above a bottom toolbar and then lists the first item nearest the button (the mockups draw what the user sees). Android: M3 Expressive menu, a segment per section, anchored to the button. Titles are complete ("Share PDF", not "Share"); no section titles. Sections: the format of the app first (Cardwallet: images; Scanner: PDF), then the other, then settings. An item that cannot apply is hidden, not disabled.
  • Values, per-context item lists, what each item does and the mapping to the code are in cardwallet-crop-export.md. The mockup is schematic (300 wide): take numbers from the markdown.
