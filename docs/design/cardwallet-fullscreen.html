Fullscreen viewer for a code (white, full-bleed, QR and wide barcode in landscape) and for photos (dark, single, several, zoomed with hidden chrome), on iOS and Android.

  • Code: white full-bleed whatever the theme, screen at max brightness and kept awake, the code at 80% of the shorter side, the value under it (selectable, 22 bold, black). Swipe between codes, dots at the bottom. A wide barcode (EAN, Code 128, PDF417) turns the screen to landscape and fills 90% of the width.
  • Photo: black full-bleed (white on e-ink), aspect fit, pinch and double tap to zoom, swipe between photos. The status bar is light.
  • Chrome: a 44 close button top-left, and for photos an edit button top-right. iOS: system glass buttons (clear glass over a photo, plain gray fill over the white code). Android: plain 24 icons in the content color, no fill. 16 from the sides, 8 below the status bar.
  • Indicator: codes use dots (selected dot in black, others at 25%). Photos use a counter pill "2 / 5", 12 above the bottom inset: glass on iOS, white 18% tonal on Android. Hidden when there is only one item.
  • Zoomed: a tap hides the chrome and the counter, a second tap brings them back. They also hide while zoomed in.
  • Back: the close button, the Android back gesture and the iOS edge swipe all dismiss. Dismissing restores the previous brightness.
