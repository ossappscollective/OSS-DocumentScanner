Page edit screen on iOS and Android: default, adjust sheet (transparent glass over the page), adjust sheet when the original image is gone, and the more menu.

  • Bars: native top bar and bottom toolbar, like the card view. Top bar: back, the document name with "Page 2 of 5" as subtitle, share, more. Bottom toolbar: the tools that change the page (crop, rotate left, rotate right, adjust, detect text, detect barcode). Everything else (PDF export, card color, detected text, barcodes, rename, delete page) is in the more menu, delete last in red.
  • Page: the page image on the neutral page color (the scanner has no card color), aspect fit, 16 margin, 6 radius, level 1 shadow on iOS and none on Android. Pinch zoom and swipe between pages. The counter pill "2 / 5" floats 12 above the toolbar: glass on iOS, tonal on Android; hidden for one page.
  • No original: crop is hidden (it needs the original image) and the Transformations group is not in the adjust sheet; only adjustments and filters remain.
  • Adjust sheet: the transparent sheet of the camera: no dim behind, forced dark, grabber, detents half and large, content on the on-glass roles. At the half detent the page scales to stay fully visible above the sheet. Sections: Transformations (switches, trailing, on both platforms), Adjustments (brightness and contrast sliders, the value on the right, tap the value to reset), Filters (a row of 56 tiles, the selected one has a 2 accent ring). Every change applies live; there is no Save. Closing the sheet keeps the changes.
  • More menu: iOS system menu from the more button; Android M3 Expressive menu, 28 radius, rows 48, delete in its own segment.
  • Controls over the page follow the Document Scanner rule: glass on iOS, tonal on Android, never over the document itself (the counter sits below the page area, not on it).
