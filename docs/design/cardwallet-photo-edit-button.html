Five placements of the photo edit button on the card view, on iOS and Android: overlay in the corner of the cell, labeled button under the photo, button on the thumbnail row, overlay inside the photo bounds, and no button (menu and fullscreen only).

  • Decided: D, with the photo tile at a constant card ratio so the button has stable bounds (rules, section 23). The notes below keep the other options.
  • A. Corner of the cell (built): the photo is aspect fit and narrower than its cell when it is not 1.58, so the button floats off the image on iOS and hangs over its edge on Android. Not recommended.
  • B. Labeled button under the photo: tonal pill, 36 tall, icon 18 and the label "adjust" (iOS: tinted capsule, Android: tonal). It never covers the image, says what it edits, and is the only photo action next to the photo. Costs 36 + 8 of height (the toolbar area is reused, no scrolling needed).
  • C. On the thumbnail row: a 36 round icon button at the right end of the strip. No extra height, no label, and it collides with the thumbnails beyond 5 photos.
  • D. Inside the image, sized to it: the same round button at 12 from the photo's own bottom-right corner (iOS clear glass, Android tonal). Best look, but the pager must expose the image bounds (the image size is derived from its aspect ratio), which is fragile on both platforms.
  • E. No button: photo edit stays in the long-press menu of a photo ("Edit photo") and in the pencil of the fullscreen viewer; tap a photo opens fullscreen. The toolbar is share and edit (card). Cleanest screen; photo edit is two taps away and less discoverable.
  • System design fit: iOS prefers a visible text control for a distinct mode ("Edit" in Photos) or a context menu (E); an unlabeled glass icon over content is for media controls only. Material 3 puts actions about a media item in the item's own row or below it (B, C) and uses an icon over the image only for media controls such as fullscreen. So B or E follow the platform conventions best; D is acceptable if the bounds can be read reliably.
